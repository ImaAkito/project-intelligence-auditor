# PROJECT COMMERCIALIZATION

## Commercial Summary

State whether the project currently has a credible path to a paid offering and the confidence of that conclusion.

## Target Customer / ICP

Describe the most plausible buyer/user segments and what remains unverified.

## Problem and Value Proposition

Explain the problem severity, current alternatives, and the value proposition supported by evidence.

## Competitive Position

Assess differentiation, technical moat, data moat, workflow moat, switching costs, and what competitors could reproduce quickly.

## Monetization Hypotheses

List plausible models such as license, subscription, API usage, enterprise deployment, services, hardware margin, or open-core where applicable. Mark them as hypotheses unless validated.

## Technology → Product Gaps

List what prevents the current technology from functioning as a coherent product.

## Product → Commercial Gaps

List what prevents the product from becoming a credible paid offering.

## Commercial → Scale Gaps

List what prevents repeatable sales, deployment, support, and delivery economics.

## Development Directions

Compare realistic strategic directions using technical fit, reuse, additional development, risk, differentiation, defensibility, time to MVP, time to revenue, and strategic value.

## Route to First Revenue

Define the shortest credible path to the first validated paid use, including what evidence must be collected before investing heavily.

## Commercial Risks

Include market, pricing, support, regulatory/legal, vendor, cost-structure, and adoption risks as applicable.
